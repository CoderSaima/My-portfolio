My Story .

"Growing up, computers were never my thing..."

If you had told me a few years ago that I would be a Software Engineer, I wouldn't have believed you. My heart was set on becoming a doctor, and computers honestly didn't interest me. But looking back, I realize that Allah perfectly directed my path to where my true potential could flourish.

Entering my BSSE degree, terms like 'coding' and 'programming' were an entirely foreign language to me. But sitting in my first-semester programming class, something magical happened.

As I watched logic translate into action, my mind raced with excitement: I think everything is possible now. I became obsessed with going deeper, searching for the roots of structural problems. I still remember the absolute joy of building my first ATM machine workflow seeing how data beautifully flowed from input to output. That was the moment I found true inner satisfaction.

That curiosity quickly became a driving force. From the third semester onwards, I threw myself into web development, cloning existing platforms to master layout and state management. Today, I can confidently build the web from scratch, translating complex backend logic into seamless digital experiences.
